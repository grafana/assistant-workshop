# Welcome

Welcome to this Grafana Labs Introduction to Grafana Assistant workshop.

You will be provided the following:

- A personal logon (username and password)
- A URL to your own Grafana Cloud stack - e.g. `https://nnnnnn.grafana.net`
- Access to the e-commerce storefront running on that stack
- A URL to your own browser-based IDE, used for the command-line part of Lab 3

:::info
Your logon has been sent to the email address you provided when you signed up. If you haven't received the email, please check your Spam folder, or let your facilitator know.
:::

## What's covered

The workshop builds from everyday fundamentals to a full incident, then out to the rest of your team's workflow, and finishes by letting the Assistant do the teaching.

### Lab 1 - Understanding Assistant

- Asking questions about your environment in natural language
- Navigating Grafana by asking instead of clicking through menus
- Finding dashboards by what they mean rather than their exact name
- Asking one broad question about a system's health, and having it answered across metrics, logs, traces, and profiles

### Lab 2 - Assistant During an Incident

- Getting your bearings in an unfamiliar system with infrastructure Memories
- Triaging by asking for the insight, rather than hunting for the dashboard that holds it
- Confirming the root cause with a Deep Investigation
- Remediating through the Assistant's connected tools, approving each action as it goes
- Turning the whole shift into reusable Rules and Skills

### Lab 3 - Assistant Where Your Team Works

- Inside an application's own interface, using the SDK
- On the command line, the way a pipeline would drive it
- In Automations that run on a schedule with nobody watching

## Lab 4 - Learning Grafana With Assistant

- Describing your role and your goals so a lesson is built from your own services
- Working through that lesson a step at a time, approving anything it wants to create
